# Presentation guide

This is a suggested walkthrough, not a completed presentation recording. The assessment brief does not state a recording length. Check Blackboard or seminar guidance before recording and adjust the timing below.

## Suggested five to six minute walkthrough

### Opening and problem

Introduce the project as an independent research proposal for stock allocation in IKEA retail operations managed by Ingka Group. Explain the trade-off between local availability, excess inventory and unnecessary transfers. Show the documented customer problem and distinguish the lack of public stockout figures from evidence that the issue exists.

### Technology and existing practice

Explain that IKEA already has AI demand sensing and automated inventory checks. The proposal extends that foundation into planner-reviewed allocation decisions. Compare the threshold baseline with machine-learning forecasting and constrained allocation. Explain why a permissioned blockchain is deferred for an internal pilot.

### Demonstration

Open the default demand-surge scenario. Show the local stock, demand uplift and donor reserve. Describe why the calculated transfer protects a fictional contribution margin. Record a simulated approval. Change to the late-transfer scenario and explain why an alert does not imply a feasible transfer. Show the limited-donor scenario, which protects the donor from being emptied. State clearly that the browser uses deterministic arithmetic and simulated values, not a trained model or real IKEA information.

### Customised maturity model

Describe the five adapted levels and five dimensions. Explain that no current IKEA scores have been invented. The pilot requires evidence on data quality, inventory visibility, decision quality, people and governance. A weak critical dimension blocks deployment even if the forecast looks good.

### Evaluation and reflection

Explain shadow testing, time-based validation, business outcomes and total costs. Discuss human oversight and the possibility that transfers increase emissions. Summarise the role of the planner. End by explaining which AI assistance was used, which claims were checked and what you personally changed or rejected.
